# FAQ

### Does changing preset delete my content?
No. Presets only change theme-wide design settings (colours, typography,
layout, cards, buttons). Your sections, menus, logo, pages and products are
untouched. Any colours or fonts you had customised in **Theme settings** are
replaced, so note them first.

### Can I use my own colours instead of a preset's?
Yes. Pick the closest preset, then change any individual setting under
**Theme settings**. The preset is only a starting point.

### How does dark mode decide which palette to show?
By your **Default mode** setting: `System` follows the visitor's device;
`Light` / `Dark` force one. If the switcher is enabled, the visitor's manual
choice wins and can be remembered on their device.

### Do I have to support dark mode?
If you set **Default mode** to `Light` and hide the switcher, the store is
light-only. You should still set sensible dark values in case you enable it
later, but visitors will not see them.

### Which fonts are available?
Anything in Shopify's font picker: the system stack, web-safe families, and
the Google Fonts library. System fonts add no download.

### Does the theme support filtering on collection pages?
Yes, via Shopify's native Search & Discovery filters. Enable **filtering** and
**sorting** on the Collection products section and configure filters in the
Search & Discovery app.

### Does it work with my apps?
The theme supports theme app blocks. Use the **Apps** section, the **Flexible
blocks** section, or the app block slot on the product page to place app
content. Older apps that inject via ScriptTag also work.

### Can I show product specs from metafields?
Yes. Add a **Flexible blocks** section to the product template and use the
**Metafield accordion** block.

### How do I add analytics?
For a simple page-view tag, use **Theme settings → SEO and tracking → Google
tag ID**. For conversion tracking use Shopify custom pixels or an analytics
app — do not put checkout tracking in the theme.
